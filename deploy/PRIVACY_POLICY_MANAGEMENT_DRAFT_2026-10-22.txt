2. Information we use
We use registration and contact details, verification/authentication records,
resumes and optional profile photographs, employment and education history,
skills, career preferences, profile edits, visibility choices, submission records,
support correspondence and necessary service/security records. Some profile
information is extracted from your resume; other information is entered by you.
Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information, identity documents and third-party
references/contact details in uploads. [DECIDE treatment of unsolicited sensitive
information and a safe removal process; do not promise automatic redaction.]

3. Purposes and account operation
We use this information to verify and secure your account, provide password
recovery, store and process your resume, let you review/edit/save your profile,
respond to requests and operate the service. [LEGAL REVIEW: map each purpose to
the relevant lawful basis; do not present blanket consent as the basis for all use.]
Authentication uses necessary cookies/tokens. [VERIFY the production cookie,
storage, telemetry and external-asset inventory before publishing specific claims.]

4. Resume processing
Uploaded PDF/DOCX files are privately stored and scanned by ClamAV. A queued worker
uses rule-based processing to extract profile information. External resume AI is
disabled: the pilot does not send resumes to an external AI extraction service.
Extraction can be incomplete or incorrect. Review and correct the information
before submitting. No automated hiring or employment decision is promised.
Any future external AI processing requires a separately approved privacy/provider
and consent review; these Terms/notice do not authorize it in advance.

5. Candidate-only visibility and later recruiter access
Recruiter access is disabled during this pilot. Draft and paused profiles are not
available for recruiter discovery. Submitting does not publish your profile on the
open web and does not, by itself, authorize future recruiter sharing.
Before recruiter access launches, we will request a separate explicit opt-in that
identifies the sharing purpose, approved recipients, information accessible,
resume-download permissions and how to withdraw. Declining sharing will not be
treated as consent. [DECIDE service consequences, if any, of declining.]
Necessary authorized operations/support access is separate from recruiter access.
[APPROVE which personnel may access data, from which countries, and for what tasks.]

6. Providers and international handling
The current deployment uses Amazon Web Services in Sydney, Australia: EC2 hosts
the application and PostgreSQL, S3 holds private files/database backups, and SQS
queues processing. Operational alerts use SNS and are designed not to contain
candidate personal information. ClamAV scanning runs in the deployed stack.
Namecheap provides domain/DNS services. Transactional email uses configured SMTP;
SES DKIM records alone do not establish the full email processing arrangement.
[VERIFY SMTP provider and legal entity, sender, processing regions, contracts,
subprocessors, email logs/retention, operational access and external media providers.]
Data may be processed outside a candidate's home country. Do not claim all data
stays in Australia, adequacy, approved safeguards or regulatory compliance until
legal review identifies the actual flows and necessary arrangements.

7. Proposed retention — not yet implemented or finally approved
The proposed policy is to retain candidate profiles and resumes for 12 months
after the last meaningful activity, unless earlier deletion is requested or a
documented lawful exception applies. [APPROVE the definition and operation below.]
Recommended activity definition: an intentional authenticated profile edit,
resume upload, submission, or explicit confirmation to retain the profile.
Decide whether login counts. Automated processing, backups, monitoring, marketing
email opens and recruiter views should not silently extend retention.
[DECIDE calendar-month calculation, pre-expiry notice/grace period, whether the
account also closes, treatment of paused/draft profiles and initial timestamps
for existing accounts. Do not backfill invented activity dates.]
Separate periods are required for unverified accounts, replaced/failed/quarantined
files, security/job logs, support correspondence, acceptance evidence and legal
holds. Twelve-month retention is not permission to retain every historical resume
or log for that period, nor to keep deleted data active for twelve months.

8. Requests and deletion — proposed service targets
Contact sheba@enterrecruitment.com. We propose acknowledging requests within two
business days and targeting active-data deletion within seven days after necessary
verification. We aim to complete or respond to verified requests within 30 days,
subject to the applicable legal deadline, including any earlier deadline. For UK
requests, use the applicable one-calendar-month rule rather than assuming 30 days
is equivalent. Extensions/exceptions require a lawful basis and timely explanation.
[APPROVE business-day calendar, reasonable identity checks, receipt/deadline
tracking, responsible owner, escalation and jurisdiction-specific rights wording.]
The existing authenticated deletion flow requires password confirmation and typing
DELETE. It revokes account access and queues associated storage cleanup; outages
can delay file removal. Manual requests need a documented, verified workflow.
Explain any justified refusal/partial retention and applicable complaint route.
Do not require excessive identification or claim a verified request always resets
every statutory clock. Access/correction/export and appeals must be reviewed for
the candidate's jurisdiction; self-service export is not currently verified.

9. Backups and recovery copies
Restricted encrypted backups may retain historical information until approved
expiry. Proposed backup retention is seven days for hourly and 30 days for daily
copies; neither is approved or enabled by this draft. Older backups need separate
disposition. Backups are not used to reactivate deleted profiles or for recruiter
discovery. Deletion decisions must be reapplied before a restored system reopens.
Private resume recovery/version retention is still awaiting approval. Database
backups do not recover deleted S3 files. [APPROVE bounded recovery-copy retention,
all-version erasure, deletion ledger, legal-hold process and owner.]
Do not promise immediate erasure of immutable/recovery copies or a fixed maximum
until the actual lifecycle and older-copy inventory support that promise.

Private storage, access controls and encrypted transport reduce risk; no absolute
security or uptime guarantee is made. [APPROVE incident response/escalation and
