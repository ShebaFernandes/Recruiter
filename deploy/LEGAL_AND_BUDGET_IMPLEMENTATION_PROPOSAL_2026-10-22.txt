4. WHY USD40 IS NOT YET A SAFE GUARANTEE
Existing8GiB host snapshot: ~5.49GiB available, ~9.72MiB DB, ~15.56GiB disk free.
This is idle/headroom evidence, not a peak-load benchmark. Container limits include
ClamAV3GiB, DB1GiB, backend1GiB, worker1GiB, plus edge/frontend and OS. Limits are
ceilings, not reserved usage, but concurrent peaks can exceed a4GiB machine.
The approved backup guard requires MORE THAN3GiB available RAM, >5GiB free disk
and DB<50MiB. An active4GiB host is unlikely to pass unchanged. Restore uses768MiB
memory limit and384MiB tmpfs. Do NOT lower these safety guards to fit a price.
ClamAV signature updates, malicious/large documents, parser subprocesses and
backup restores must coexist without OOM-killing PostgreSQL or dropping jobs.
Lightsail is a migration, not an EC2 resize; existing EC2 instance-role credentials,
pinned instance/account guards and installation paths cannot be assumed portable.
Require a supported temporary-credential design for S3/SQS/SNS; do not replace the
role with embedded long-lived access keys. Migration effort/temporary overlap extra.
ARM needs new arm64 images/scans/tests; current approved images are amd64. Spot is
not recommended for the sole stateful database/application host. Stopping nightly
would break24/7 availability, queues and scheduled protection. Disabling ClamAV,
backups, TLS or private storage is not an acceptable budget measure.
