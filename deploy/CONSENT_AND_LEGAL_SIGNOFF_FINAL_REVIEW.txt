  exclude resume bodies, credentials and tokens; erase or irreversibly aggregate.

  indefinitely under an archive label.

  of deletion at an exact time. Three legacy copies remain outside these prefixes
  under a management-approved protection hold. Their future disposition requires
  separate authorization after recovery verification and is not a launch condition.
Resume versions (optional recovery choice) | Noncurrent versions: 7 days from
  replacement/deletion | Bounded accidental-loss recovery; separate approval needed
