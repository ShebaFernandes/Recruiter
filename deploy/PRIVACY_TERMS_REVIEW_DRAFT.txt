Factual privacy wording for owner/counsel review
ENTER collects account/contact information, resumes, profile and career preferences,
and your edits and submission choices to operate its recruitment platform. Uploaded
documents are stored privately and scanned for malware before asynchronous parsing.
The current resume parser is rule-based; external AI resume processing is disabled.
You can review and correct extracted information before submitting your profile.
Submission records a consent timestamp and version. Draft/paused profiles are not
intended for recruiter discovery. Candidate-only launch disables recruiter access;
any future recruiter rollout must be consistent with the consent you were shown.
Do not promise that submitting guarantees employment, introductions or accuracy.

Hosting currently uses AWS Sydney EC2, PostgreSQL on EC2, private S3 storage,
SQS processing queues and ClamAV scanning. Email uses a configured SMTP provider;
its legal entity, processing locations, terms and retention must be confirmed.
List actual subprocessors, transfers, backup locations and support access. Do not
describe all data as Australia-only until email/support/telemetry flows are verified.
Authentication uses secure cookies/tokens. Describe necessary authentication storage
and any actual analytics separately; do not invent advertising/analytics use.

You may request/delete your account through the authenticated account interface.
Deletion requires password confirmation; account access is revoked and file cleanup
is queued. Storage outages may delay cleanup and require operator follow-up. Recovery
copies may persist under a defined, access-restricted backup retention policy until
expiry. State the approved timing, erasure exceptions and complaint route; do not
promise instant removal from backups or unlimited retention.
