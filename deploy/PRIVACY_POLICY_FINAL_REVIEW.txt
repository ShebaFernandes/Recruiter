2. Scope and information we handle

This policy applies to the Candidate Platform. It does not authorize recruiter
access, external resume-AI processing or public publication of candidate profiles.

We handle:

- account and contact details, email-verification state and authentication records;
- uploaded PDF or DOCX resumes and optional profile photographs;
- employment, education, skills, achievements, career preferences and settings;
- rule-based information extracted from a resume and corrections made by you;
- draft, paused and submitted profile state, consent and document-acceptance records;
- support, privacy and deletion correspondence; and
- security, audit, queue, backup and service-operation records needed to run and
  protect the platform.

Information comes from you, from rule-based extraction of files you upload and
from your use of the service. Do not include identity documents, unnecessary
sensitive information or another person’s private contact details in a resume.

If an uploaded document nevertheless contains health, disability, racial or ethnic
origin, religious belief, trade-union, sexual-life, criminal-offence or similarly
protected information, ENTER will not intentionally use it for recruiter matching
or disclose it to recruiters during the candidate-only pilot. It may still be
present in the privately stored file and pass through security scanning and
rule-based parsing. Ask us to correct or delete it. Any future use requiring a
special legal condition or explicit consent must be reviewed and authorized first.

3. Why and on what basis we use information

We use candidate information to:

- create, verify, authenticate and secure an account;
- receive, scan, store and process a resume at the candidate’s request;
- let the candidate review, correct, maintain, preview and submit a profile;
- remember career settings and candidate choices;
- provide support, password recovery and privacy-request handling;
- detect abuse, malware and unauthorized access, maintain service availability,
  investigate incidents and keep necessary recovery records; and
- comply with applicable law and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Where UK data-protection law applies, the proposed bases are steps requested before
and performance of the candidate service contract for core account/profile
functions; ENTER’s legitimate interests in securing and administering the service
where those interests are not overridden; legal obligations where applicable; and
consent for genuinely optional processing that requires it. Future recruiter
sharing requires a separate, explicit opt-in. Protected-category information will
not be used unless an applicable additional condition has been identified.

In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025 have
phased commencement. The Act received assent on 11 August 2023. Under G.S.R.
843(E) and G.S.R. 846(E), the first phase commenced on 13 November 2025, the
one-year phase is scheduled for 13 November 2026, and the core processing,
candidate-rights, security and breach provisions are scheduled eighteen months
after publication, on 13 May 2027. ENTER will apply the operational commitments
in this policy from its effective date and must review the service before each
later phase begins. This statement does not claim that provisions not yet
commenced are already legally in force.

In the United States, privacy obligations differ by state and by statutory scope
and thresholds. ENTER will honor the controls described in this policy and any
additional rights that apply to a particular candidate. ENTER does not claim that
every US state law applies to it, or that this policy exhausts applicable law.

4. Resume processing and candidate review

Resumes are held in private storage and scanned using ClamAV. A queue and worker
run a rule-based parser to create suggested profile fields. External resume AI is
disabled, and this policy does not authorize sending resumes to an external AI
extraction provider. Extraction can omit or misread information; candidates must
be able to review and correct the result before submission. ENTER does not make
solely automated employment decisions through this processing. Submission does
not guarantee an interview, employment or placement.

5. Visibility and future recruiter access

Recruiter login, recruiter APIs and recruiter resume access are disabled during
the candidate-only pilot. Draft, paused and submitted profiles are not made
available for recruiter discovery. A submitted profile is not published on the
open web.

Terms acceptance and profile submission are not permission for recruiter sharing.
Before any recruiter feature launches, ENTER will present a separate, unchecked
opt-in explaining the approved recruiter recipients, purposes, visible fields,
resume-download access, withdrawal process and the limits of withdrawal for any
copy already lawfully received. Declining that future opt-in will not by itself
remove a candidate’s ability to maintain or delete their account.

Only specifically authorized administrators and support, security or operations
personnel may access candidate information, on a need-to-know basis for a defined
purpose. ENTER’s approved operating policy is India-only staff access. Before
publication, ENTER must verify that personnel assignments, credentials and access
configuration comply; the policy cannot make that factual claim solely from this
management decision.

6. Cookies, local storage and external assets

The platform uses an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax enter_session cookie for
authentication and a csrftoken cookie for cross-site-request-forgery protection.
Browser session storage holds unsaved candidate answers and an intro-animation
replay flag until the tab/session is cleared. These are used to provide and secure
the service, not for advertising.

The reviewed production build did not contain a dedicated analytics SDK. It loads
a background video from d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net. That request provides the
delivery service with ordinary connection information such as IP address, browser
details and request time. ENTER does not claim that infrastructure providers keep
no technical logs. If optional analytics or advertising technology is introduced,
ENTER must update this policy and obtain any required choice before activating it.

8. Candidate profile and resume retention

ENTER retains a candidate profile and current resume for 12 calendar months after
meaningful activity. It sends reminders 30 days and seven days before expiry.
Earlier verified deletion requests take precedence.

For this policy, meaningful activity is a
candidate-initiated saved profile or career-settings change, accepted resume
upload, profile submission or explicit renewal. Passive login, email opening,
background processing, worker retries and administrative changes do not reset the
clock. The clock is calculated in UTC and applies to draft, submitted and paused
profiles. A failed reminder is escalated to the privacy owner and does not reset
activity. No additional grace period is added after the 12 months.

For an existing account without reliable activity evidence, ENTER will not invent
an activity date. The approved transition is an explicit renewal request and a
one-time 30-day notice before expiry processing. At expiry, ordinary processing is
restricted and verified active-data deletion begins, subject only to an applicable
legal obligation documented by the privacy owner. Automatic expiry deletion will
not be activated until this transition and the complete deletion procedure have
been separately tested and approved.

9. Other retention periods

ENTER has approved the following retention periods. Automated enforcement remains
disabled until the corresponding procedure is tested and separately activated:

- unverified registrations: 30 days after registration;
- a replaced resume and failed non-malicious upload: seven days after successful
  replacement or final failure, once recovery and cleanup checks pass;
- malware-quarantined material: up to 30 days after quarantine, inaccessible to
  ordinary users, unless a shorter security procedure safely disposes of it;
- security and processing logs: 12 months from the event, or longer only when an
  applicable law or documented investigation requires it;
- closed support correspondence: 12 months after closure;
- Terms, age and submission evidence: while the account is active and 12 months
  after account closure, unless an applicable claim or law requires longer; and
- the deletion ledger: 12 months after verified active deletion and until no
  retained database backup can restore the deleted record, whichever is later.

Routine encrypted database backups under postgres/hourly/ expire after seven days
and those under postgres/daily/ after 30 days. S3 expiration is asynchronous. The
old broad 14-day rule is disabled. Three legacy backups outside those prefixes
remain protected under a management-approved hold. No deletion is authorized;
any later disposition requires separate authorization after recovery verification.
Resume-bucket versioning is disabled, and database backups do not restore deleted
resume objects.

10. Candidate rights, requests and complaints

Candidates may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection,
portability or other rights available under applicable law. Rights depend on the
jurisdiction and processing basis. Contact sheba@enterrecruitment.com. ENTER may
request proportionate proof of account control but will not routinely request an
identity document.

ENTER’s operational target is to acknowledge a deletion request within two
business days and delete active account/profile/resume data within seven days
after a verified request. “Business day” means Monday to Friday, excluding dates
in ENTER’s approved Karnataka public-holiday calendar. The verified 2026 calendar
must be loaded into the operating configuration before deadline automation. A legal response clock is not
silently restarted after identity verification; where the law starts it at a
different point, the legal rule prevails. Cleanup failures are tracked and
escalated to Dikshita Verma.

For UK candidates where UK data-protection law applies, requests are handled
without undue delay and normally within one calendar month, subject to any lawful
extension or exception. Candidates may complain to ENTER first and may also
complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. UK complaint-handling duties
in force from June 2026, including a clear complaint route and acknowledgement
within 30 days, will be reflected in ENTER’s operating procedure.

For Indian candidates, ENTER will provide the grievance and rights routes required
by each commenced phase of the DPDP Act and Rules. As of this review, the core
rights and grievance provisions are scheduled for 13 May 2027; the policy must be
rechecked before that date. For US candidates, ENTER will honor access, correction,
deletion, appeal, opt-out or regulator-contact rights when an applicable federal
or state law requires them. Applicability depends on facts and statutory thresholds.

11. Deletion, backups and restoration

The authenticated deletion flow requires password confirmation and the literal
word DELETE. Email requests use proportionate account-control verification. Active
account access is revoked and private-file cleanup is queued. Completion is
recorded only after storage absence is verified; failed cleanup is reported without
exposing candidate information.

Encrypted backups may temporarily retain historical database records after active
deletion. Backups remain access restricted and expire under the approved lifecycle.
ENTER does not promise immediate erasure from every recovery copy. A verified
deletion ledger must be applied to an isolated restore before accounts, tokens or
recruiter discovery can be enabled. Restoration fails closed if the ledger is
missing or invalid. This prevents a recovery from silently reactivating deleted
accounts, but it is not proof that every historical copy has already been erased.

12. Security and incidents

ENTER uses private storage, access controls, encrypted transport, malware scanning,
monitoring and encrypted backups. No system can guarantee absolute security or
uninterrupted availability. ENTER will investigate a suspected personal-data
incident, contain it, preserve necessary evidence and notify affected people and
authorities when applicable law requires. The privacy owner coordinates the
response; the exact regional notification analysis is made for the incident and
is not replaced by a generic promise in this policy.

13. Policy changes

ENTER will retain immutable, dated policy versions and the exact hash of the text
presented for acceptance. Material changes will be communicated before the new
use begins and will trigger reacceptance where required. Privacy notice is not
retrospective blanket consent, and no change can infer permission for recruiter
sharing. The current version and effective date will be displayed on this page.
