3. How we use information
We use information to provide and secure your account, verify your email, support
password recovery, process your resume, let you review and maintain your profile,
respond to requests and operate the platform. Necessary authentication cookies
and tokens support account access.
The application uses the enter_session authentication cookie and csrftoken CSRF
cookie. Local browser session storage holds unsaved candidate answers and an
intro-animation replay flag. The background video is requested from
d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net; that request necessarily exposes connection metadata
such as IP address to the delivery service. No dedicated analytics SDK was found
in the reviewed frontend source. This is not a claim that providers keep no logs.

4. Resume processing and your review
Uploaded PDF and DOCX resumes are privately stored and scanned using ClamAV.
Queued processing uses a rule-based parser to extract profile information.
External resume AI processing is disabled; resumes are not sent to an external
AI extraction service under this pilot configuration.
Extraction may omit or misinterpret information. You can review and correct your
profile before submitting it. Submission does not guarantee employment or placement.
Any future external AI processing requires a separate review and appropriate
notice and permissions; this policy does not authorize it in advance.

5. Visibility and future recruiter access
Recruiter access is disabled during the candidate-only pilot. Draft and paused
profiles are not available for recruiter discovery. Submitting your profile does
not publish it on the open web or grant permission for future recruiter sharing.
Before making your profile available to recruiters, ENTER will ask for a separate,
explicit opt-in explaining recipients, purposes, accessible information, resume
downloads and how to withdraw. Existing submission consent or Terms acceptance
will not be treated as that future permission.
Authorized operational/support access is distinct from recruiter access.

9. Backups and recovery
Encrypted database backups can contain historical personal information after
active records are deleted. Backup handling must remain access-restricted and
consistent with the final approved retention and deletion procedure.
Verified operational configuration, October 22, 2026: S3 expiration is enabled
for postgres/hourly/ after seven days and postgres/daily/ after 30 days. The old
14-day postgres/ rule is disabled. Expiration is asynchronous, not a guarantee
of erasure at an exact deadline. Three existing legacy backups outside those
scheduled prefixes remain retained pending an explicit disposition decision.
This records current configuration, not complete-policy approval or proof that
recurring backup timers are active. Resume-bucket versioning remains disabled.
[D10: approve legacy-copy disposition, resume-recovery choice and the remaining
