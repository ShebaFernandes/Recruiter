ENTER Candidate Platform Privacy Policy

1. Who we are and who this service is for

ENTER Recruitment Private Limited (“ENTER”, “we”, “us”) operates the ENTER

Registered address: Regent Insignia, KHB Block Koramangala, 5th Block,
Koramangala, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560034, India.
Corporate Identity Number: U74999DL2020PTC370862.

Dikshita Verma, CEO, is ENTER's privacy owner. Contact
sheba@enterrecruitment.com for privacy questions, requests, complaints, or

New registration is limited to people aged 18 or over who select India as their
registration country. The country selection is not proof of physical location.
Recruiter access and sharing remain disabled. Optional external resume-AI
processing is available only when a candidate selects the separate, unchecked
permission shown with that specific resume upload. This policy does not authorize
public profiles, recruiter access, advertising use, or sale of candidate
information.

2. Information we handle

We handle the information needed to provide and protect your candidate account:

- your name, email address, India registration selection, email-verification and
  authentication records;
- your PDF or DOCX resume, optional profile photograph, work and education
  history, skills, achievements, career preferences, and settings;
- rule-based resume-extraction results and, when you separately choose it for a
  specific upload, the resume text sent to Google Gemini and the structured
  extraction returned to ENTER;
- draft, paused, and submitted profile state, and records of age confirmation,
  Terms acceptance, and profile-submission consent;
- support, privacy, complaint, and deletion correspondence; and
- limited security, audit, processing, backup, and service-operation records.

The information comes from you, the files you upload, and your use of the
service. Please do not upload identity documents, unnecessary sensitive
information, or another person's private details. If a resume nevertheless
contains sensitive information, it may remain in the private file and pass
through malware scanning and rule-based parsing. We do not use it for recruiter
matching during the candidate-only launch. You may ask us to correct or delete it.

3. Why we use information

We use candidate information to create, verify, authenticate, and secure your
account; receive, scan, store, and process your resume; let you review, correct,
save, preview, and submit your profile; provide password recovery and support;
honour privacy and deletion requests; prevent abuse and investigate incidents;
maintain and recover the service; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Resume extraction uses ENTER's rule-based parser by default. If you select the
optional AI permission for a specific upload, ENTER sends the text extracted from
that resume to Google's paid Gemini API to identify professional titles, summaries,
employers, roles, employment dates, skills, and evidence-backed achievements for
your profile. ENTER removes recognized direct contact details before sending the
text where practical, but employment history and other personal information
contained in the resume may remain. Gemini's suggestions are evidence-validated,
may still be incomplete or wrong, and must remain reviewable and correctable by
the candidate. ENTER does not use this processing to make a solely automated
hiring decision and does not guarantee an interview, placement, or job.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data
Protection Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in phases. ENTER applies the
commitments in this policy from its effective date and will review the platform
before later phases take effect. Where consent is the applicable basis, you may
withdraw it through sheba@enterrecruitment.com as easily as you gave it, without
affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Withdrawal may mean that
ENTER can no longer provide the affected part of the service.

4. Candidate control and recruiter sharing

Your account is private. Recruiter login, search, candidate-profile access, and
resume downloads are disabled. A submitted profile is not published on the open
web and submission is not permission for recruiter sharing.

Before any recruiter feature is enabled, ENTER will ask for a separate, unchecked
opt-in that identifies the approved recipients, purpose, visible information,
download access, and withdrawal process. Declining that future opt-in does not
prevent you from maintaining or deleting your candidate account.

Only specifically authorized operations, support, security, or administrative
personnel may access candidate information when necessary for a defined task.

5. Storage, providers, cookies, and security

Resumes are stored privately, encrypted at rest, and scanned with ClamAV. The
platform uses encrypted HTTPS transport, access controls, malware scanning,
monitoring, and protected backups. No system can guarantee absolute security or
uninterrupted availability. ENTER will investigate incidents and notify affected
people or authorities when applicable law requires.

The platform uses a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax authentication cookie and a
CSRF-protection cookie. Browser session storage temporarily holds unsaved answers
and an intro-animation setting. The reviewed candidate launch has no advertising
or analytics SDK. A background video delivered from an Amazon CloudFront hostname
causes ordinary connection information, such as IP address, browser details, and
request time, to be sent to that delivery service.

The service uses Amazon Web Services in Sydney, Australia for application and
database hosting, private file storage, queues, transactional email, operational
alerts, and encrypted database backups; ClamAV in ENTER's deployment for malware
scanning; Namecheap for domain, DNS, and inbound email forwarding; and Amazon
CloudFront for the background video. These services may process technical or
candidate information outside India. ENTER limits them to the service they
provide and will use any safeguard required by applicable law.

For a resume whose candidate selected the optional AI permission, ENTER uses
Google's paid Gemini API as an external processing provider. The extracted resume
text and Gemini's structured response may be processed outside India in locations
used by Google and its subprocessors. ENTER does not send candidate resumes through
Gemini's unpaid services: Google's terms say unpaid inputs and outputs may be used
to improve its products and may be reviewed by people. Google states that, for its
Paid Services, prompts and responses are not used to improve its products and are
processed under Google's applicable Data Processing Addendum. ENTER disables
optional request logging and storage, does not contribute prompts, responses,
feedback, or datasets to Google, and applies Google's then-current paid-service,
abuse-monitoring, retention, and data-processing terms. Limited processing or
retention required for security, abuse prevention, law, or an agreed service
feature may still apply.

6. Retention, deletion, and recovery

ENTER retains your current candidate profile and resume for 12 calendar months
after meaningful activity, unless you request earlier deletion or a documented
legal requirement applies. Meaningful activity is a candidate-initiated saved
profile or career-setting change, accepted resume upload, profile submission, or
explicit renewal. Passive login, email opening, background processing, and
administrative activity do not reset the period. ENTER sends reminders about 30
days and seven days before inactivity expiry.

Unverified registrations are retained for 30 days. Replaced resumes and failed
non-malicious uploads are retained for up to seven days after safe replacement or
final failure; malware-quarantined files for up to 30 days; security and
processing logs and closed support correspondence for 12 months; and acceptance
evidence while the account is active and for 12 months after closure, unless a
documented claim or legal obligation requires longer.

You may delete your account through the authenticated account flow or contact
sheba@enterrecruitment.com. ENTER's service target is to acknowledge a verified
deletion request within two business days and delete active account, profile, and
resume data within seven days. A business day is Monday to Friday excluding the
approved Karnataka public-holiday calendar. We may request proportionate evidence
of account control and will explain a lawful exception or delay.

Routine encrypted hourly database backups expire after seven days and daily
backups after 30 days; storage expiration may take additional time. Three older,
access-restricted database backups remain under a documented recovery hold. They
are not used for ordinary service or recruiter access. If a backup is restored,
verified deletion records are reapplied before accounts can be enabled. Database
backups do not restore deleted resume objects.

7. Your choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or receive information about your data,
withdraw an applicable consent, or exercise another right available under law.
Contact sheba@enterrecruitment.com. You may also raise a grievance or complaint
through that address and, when applicable, with the competent authority.

AI-assisted extraction is optional. Declining it does not prevent resume upload
or rule-based extraction. You may withdraw permission for future external
processing by leaving the checkbox unchecked on later uploads or by contacting
ENTER. Withdrawal cannot recall a resume request already sent to Google or
undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Deleting the resume or
account remains subject to the deletion and backup periods above.

You must separately confirm that you are at least 18 and accept the displayed
Terms version. The Privacy Policy is a notice, not blanket consent. Profile
submission requires its own confirmation. Existing accounts are not treated as
having accepted a new document merely because they used the service before.

8. Changes and contact

ENTER keeps dated versions and a hash of the exact legal text presented for
acceptance. We will communicate material changes before the changed use begins
and obtain a new acceptance or consent where required. A policy change cannot be
used to infer permission for recruiter sharing.
