3. The candidate service

The service lets you upload a supported resume, receive rule-based extraction,

preview a resume, and save or submit a profile. Review extracted information

The candidate service is free under this version. If you select the optional AI
permission, ENTER may send the text extracted from that resume to Google's paid Gemini API

not select it, ENTER uses the rule-based parser. Recruiter login, search,
candidate access, and resume downloads remain disabled. Submission does not
authorize recruiter access. Before any future recruiter sharing, ENTER will
request a separate, explicit opt-in describing the approved recipients, purpose,

For a resume whose optional AI permission you select, that limited permission

The separately versioned Privacy Policy explains what ENTER handles, why, where,

asynchronous. Older protected backups and restoration controls are disclosed in

Terms acceptance does not waive privacy rights, authorize indefinite retention,
