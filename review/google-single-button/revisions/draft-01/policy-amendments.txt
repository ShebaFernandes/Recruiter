T1. Section 1, eligibility paragraph — replace with:

New registration is limited to individuals aged 18 or over who choose India as
their registration country. For a new Google signup, selecting “Continue with

declaration that you are at least 18 and choose India as your registration

and does not authorize recruiter sharing or optional external AI processing.

T3. Section 9, first paragraph — replace with:
