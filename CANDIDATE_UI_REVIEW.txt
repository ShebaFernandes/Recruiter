UPDATED SCREENS

Resume uploads and updates share CandidateProcessing:
- Existing mountain background and a centred, softly translucent white card.
- Exact headline: “While we get to know you…”
- Exact supporting line: “We're turning your experience into a clearer picture of your career.”
- Primary animated teal loading ring and indeterminate progress rail. No percentage,
  simulated completion or timed extraction/validation milestones.
- Actual upload, queue, scan and processing status controls the status copy.
  The API does not distinguish live extraction from validation, so the processing
  state truthfully says “extracting and validating your experience” together.
- Worker delays and status errors stop decorative animation and expose Check status
  again. Sixty seconds without a state change also pauses animation and offers the
  check, while status polling continues. Only backend completion opens My Profile.
- Four small static text chips appear below the loading section. They have no
  checkboxes, handlers, selection state, tab stops, save requests or submit button.
- Gemini consent stays separate on the upload/replacement form. Existing career
  preferences remain untouched; the loading screen no longer writes preferences.

Shared Career Journey (candidate and recruiter):
- All saved roles render with full employer names, titles and date labels.
- One uniform calendar scale positions known periods and genuine gaps. The scale
  can expand uniformly to keep successive role labels on a single readable row.
- True overlaps retain separate period tracks and individual accessible targets;
  simultaneous/very close labels can use additional rows. Promotions stay separate.
- Light teal requires supported internship evidence or candidate-confirmed type.
- Dashed gaps are conservative: unresolved dates cannot assert an employment gap.
- A single known period fills the available width. A single undated role starts at
  the left, with its supplied dates and no fabricated duration.
- Year-only dates retain year-only wording; missing end dates do not imply Present.
  Unknown/partial periods remain visible outside the precise dated axis.
- Full names and dates wrap without ellipsis. Long histories scroll horizontally.
  Hover, tap, keyboard focus and Escape details work in both audiences.

Employment status is distinct from processing completion:
- Verified against resume evidence.
- Explicit no-employment declaration in the resume.
- Extraction failed/needs review, without calling the candidate a fresher.
- Older/manual history preserved, not verified by the latest upload.
- Pending proposals are labelled as awaiting review, not already applied/verified.
- Legacy saved records without outcome metadata do not claim latest-resume verification.

The current backend's confirmed_no_employment value means an explicit statement in
resume content. It does not expose a separate “candidate clicked no employment”
confirmation flag. The UI names the available evidence honestly; empty extraction
alone never becomes a no-employment declaration.
