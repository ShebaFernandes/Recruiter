27 October 2026. Local only; no paid provider requests or production deployment.

Why the reference differed
The candidate wrapper explicitly disabled company labels, and the shared component
rendered marker-only timelines. Paid Google billing does not change those UI settings.
The internship detector only recognized titles ending in Intern/Internship or beginning
with Intern followed by a separator. AI Intern / SDE Intern - AI did not match.

Changes
- Candidate profiles and detailed recruiter profiles now show complete saved employer,
  title and month/year date summaries under the continuous horizontal timeline.
  Compact recruiter lists retain the compact interaction. Axis labels remain years-only.
- Ongoing dated employment has an arrow. The dated axis fills the available space while
  preserving a uniform calendar scale. Labels near the edge stay inside the scrollable
  canvas and use separate lanes when necessary; saved facts are never shifted in time.
- Explicit slash-separated internship titles and specialization suffixes are recognized.
  Internship line/marker styling uses light teal; other roles use dark teal. Duration,
  age and incidental descriptions of interns cannot classify a role as an internship.
  Explicit conflicting employment types remain authoritative.
- A resume end date stated as a month (e.g. Jul 2026) fills that entire month visually.
  Jan–Jul followed by Sep–Present now leaves only August as a one-month gap. Stored
  dates are unchanged. Exact ISO-date evidence keeps its exact position; incomplete
  dates remain outside the precise scale. Hover/tap/keyboard details remain available.
- The rules parser recognizes internship status from the explicit role title. For
  employer/title/date stacked layouts, the next employer/title can no longer leak
  into the previous role's description. Evidence includes the corresponding headers.
- Provenance tests now use per-test private storage, preventing reset test IDs from
  reading clean fictional files left by an earlier test run.
