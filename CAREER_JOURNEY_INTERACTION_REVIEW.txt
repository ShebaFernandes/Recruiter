Career Journey interaction review — 26 October 2026

Implemented the latest interaction direction. This supersedes the earlier
proposal to hide unverified saved roles in a separate disclosure.

Default graph shows only a continuous horizontal axis, employment markers and
year labels. Company, title, complete date labels and evidence-supported
employment type appear in the shared hover/tap/focus popover. Escape, a second
tap or an outside interaction dismisses it. Touch targets are 44px. Reduced
motion is respected. Long histories scroll within the graph at mobile widths.

Actual saved employment records supply every marker. Latest-resume verification
controls filled versus hollow markers, never removal of saved history. Manual
or candidate-confirmed history remains identifiable in the card. Only an empty
saved history produces an empty state. Failed extraction is not called fresher.

Positions share an actual calendar scale. Promotions and overlaps remain
separate. Very close/simultaneous starts retain their horizontal date position
and use extra marker lanes. Unknown/partial dates remain off the dated axis,
with the original date label and an explanation in the popover. Genuine gaps
use dashed segments; incomplete evidence and adjacent months do not imply gaps.
Verified internships use lighter teal; other roles use deeper teal. Full-time
text is shown only when supported by the saved employment evidence.
